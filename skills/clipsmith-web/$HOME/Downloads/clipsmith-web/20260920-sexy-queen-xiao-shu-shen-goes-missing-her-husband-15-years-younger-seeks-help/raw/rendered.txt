'Sexy Queen' Xiao Shu-shen goes missing, her husband, 15 years younger, seeks help.

TAIWAN (CHINA) - Xiao Shu-shen, the "Queen of Seduction" of Chinese cinema, has been missing for a week after an argument with her younger husband, prompting him to seek help online to find her.

According to HK01 , Liang Xuan'an, the husband of Xiao Shushen, who is 15 years younger than her, said that he and the actress had a big argument more than a week ago. The next morning, he went to work and returned home to find his wife was no longer there.

Xiao Shushen lost contact for a week after arguing with her younger husband.

The husband tried every way to contact her, asking relatives for help, but he had lost contact with Xiao Shushen for the past few days. He turned to the internet to "plead for help," hoping to receive assistance from everyone.

Leung Hsien-an stated that his business has recently faced difficulties following a lawsuit alleging he sexually assaulted an actress. He had previously borrowed 30,000 New Taiwan Dollars (approximately 24 million Vietnamese Dong) from Hsien-sien to cover business expenses. A few days ago, Leung Hsien-an again asked his wife if she could lend him another 30,000 New Taiwan Dollars.

"Xiao Shushen refused and then scolded me about money. She called me a 'parasite,' which angered me, and the argument escalated," he recounted.

Xiao Shu-shen and Liang Xuan-an registered their marriage in November 2017. After nine years together, the couple has faced considerable controversy. Her husband is much younger than her and had a previous marriage. The actress longs to have children with her partner, but this has yet to happen.

Xiao Shu-shen, born in 1976, was once considered a sex symbol in Taiwan. She has appeared in many famous works such as "Blood Sword," "Conspiracy and Love," "Happy March ," etc. She has also starred in music videos for a number of famous stars such as Andy Lau, Andy Hui, Kwong Leung, Jay Chou, and others.
